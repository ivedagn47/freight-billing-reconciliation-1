## What we found

The freight charge is made up of a weight component and a distance component, which together come to less than the total stated on the invoice for this line. The shipment has no special handling, so no cold-chain premium applies on top of freight. The extra amount on the invoice is not accounted for by any charge shown.

## Contract basis

Clause 1 defines freight as a weight component (₹6.00/kg of billed weight) plus a distance component (₹2.00/km of booked lane distance); clause 3's cold-chain premium does not apply as the shipment carries no special handling.

## Next step

Ask the carrier to correct the line to the contract amount shown above (the sum of the weight and distance components) or to issue a credit note for the difference, citing clause 1.
