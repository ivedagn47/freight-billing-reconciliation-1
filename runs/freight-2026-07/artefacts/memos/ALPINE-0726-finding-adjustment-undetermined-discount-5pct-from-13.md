## What we found

Alpine tendered 40 consignments in 2026-07, well over the 12-consignment threshold, so the contract's 5% monthly discount applies to this invoice. However, the invoice's overall contract total cannot currently be determined, so the discount's amount cannot be calculated or verified from this invoice as billed.

## Contract basis

Clause 5: in any calendar month in which the Customer tenders more than 12 consignments, the Carrier applies a 5% discount to the invoice total for that month. The contract does not say how to apply this once part of the invoice total is otherwise undetermined.

## Next step

Escalate to finance or the contract owner for a decision on how to apply the 5% discount once the undetermined line item(s) on this invoice are resolved; the consignment count (40) supporting the discount is not itself in question.
