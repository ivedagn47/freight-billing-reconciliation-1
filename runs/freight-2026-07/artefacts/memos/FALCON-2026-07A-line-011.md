## What we found

The line bills a detention charge at consignee in addition to freight and the fuel surcharge. The contract does not include detention among its chargeable items, so this charge is not supported. Freight and the fuel surcharge together account for the contract amount shown above; the detention charge is the source of the full difference.

## Contract basis

Clauses 1 and 3 support the base freight and fuel surcharge that make up the contract amount above. Clause 5 states no other accessorial charges are payable under the agreement, and detention is not listed among the accessorials in clause 4.

## Next step

Ask the carrier to reissue the line at the contract amount shown above, removing the detention charge, or to issue a credit note for the difference, citing clause 5 (no accessorial charges outside the schedule) and clause 8 (credit notes).
